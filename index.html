Severe Choking (Heimlich Maneuver) High Priority
  1. Verify: Ask "Are you choking?" If unable to speak or cough, act immediately.
  2. Position: Stand behind the person, wrap arms around waist, lean them slightly forward.
  3. Thrusts: Place fist thumb-side in above navel; grasp with other hand and give quick, inward-upward abdominal thrusts.
  4. Unresponsive: If victim loses consciousness, lower gently to floor and begin CPR immediately.
Severe Bleeding & Hemorrhage Critical
  1. Direct Pressure: Apply firm, steady pressure directly over the wound with sterile gauze or clean cloth.
  2. Maintain: Do not remove soaked gauze; add more layers on top and secure tightly.
  3. Tourniquet: For catastrophic limb bleeding that won't stop, apply a commercial tourniquet 2-3 inches above wound (never over a joint).
  4. Keep Warm: Treat for shock by laying victim down, elevating legs, and keeping warm with a blanket.
Stroke Detection (F.A.S.T. Protocol) Critical
  • F - Face Drooping: Ask person to smile. Does one side of the face droop or feel numb?
  • A - Arm Weakness: Ask person to raise both arms. Does one arm drift downward?
  • S - Speech Difficulty: Ask person to repeat a simple sentence. Is speech slurred or strange?
  • T - Time to Call 112/108: If any signs are positive, call emergency services immediately and note the exact time symptoms started.
Burns & Scalds Treatment Medium Priority
  1. Cool Water: Immediately cool burn under cool running tap water for at least 10–20 minutes. Do NOT use ice.
  2. Protect: Cover burn loosely with sterile non-stick bandage or clean plastic wrap.
  3. Do NOT: Never burst blisters, peel away adhered clothing, or apply toothpaste, butter, or oil.
  4. Seek Care: Seek immediate medical attention if burn is large, on face/hands, or caused by chemical/electrical source.
Fire & Smoke Evacuation High Priority
  1. Stay Low: Crawl low under smoke where air is cleaner and cooler.
  2. Check Doors: Feel door handles with the back of hand before opening; if hot, do not open.
  3. Escape: Use nearest fire stairwell. NEVER use elevators during a fire.
  4. Assemble: Reach designated evacuation assembly point outside and do not re-enter.
